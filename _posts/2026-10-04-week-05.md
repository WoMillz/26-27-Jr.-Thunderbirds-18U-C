## Coaches Corner — Last Week's Observations

### Games
First Game of the Season against Sno-King 18U Chen. 

Overall, the team did well with the shorter bench. We saw a lot of what we talked about and worked on during Thursday's practice show up in the game. Players were spreading out, moving the puck and taking shots on net. I believe shots finished at 25–23 for us which shows we didn't get out played. The biggest opportunity came in our own zone, and specifically in front of the net, where we failed to protect the house and support our goalie.

We did win the third period, going 3–0 and spending the majority of that period in Chen's end. The second goal was a battle in the corner leading to a backhand pass from Braeden to Thomas, who was right in the slot. Then we scored two goals in the final minute, about .6 seconds apart. If we can get that third-period play consistent throughout the game, we will be a tough team to play against.

## This Week's Focus

**Theme:** **Positioning & Decision Making**
- **WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN POSITION AND POSITIONING?**
	- Positioning is where you are on the ice in relation to the puck and other players
	- Position is where you line up on the face-off or what you are rostered as
- **THE FOUR ROLES**
	- Players are in one of the 4 roles of game play no matter what postion they play. Transitions between roles happens frequently and quickly.
	- The faster individuals, and teams, transition between roles, the more success they experience in games.
	- If a player or team can identify transitions, even a fraction of a second earlier than the other team, it can lead to a 5-10ft advantage in the opposite direction.
