## Coaches Corner
A competitive game against Team Scofield. A lot of things are working. Shots on net were close again at 29–30, meaning we’re getting plenty of opportunities. The coaches mentioned that the effort and competition were solid through all three periods. Great work. Despite all the positives from the game, there were still some breakdowns.

This week’s practices have continued our work on positioning while layering in some defensive best practices. Tonight’s practice is no different, focusing on protecting the house and attacking the opposing puck carrier.

Big game this weekend, as it’s our first game this season against a non-Sno-King team. I expect it to be as competitive as the Sno-King games, especially since Bremerton is known for having some very talented and challenging players on its team. We’ve got a great roster, so it will be exciting to see how the team rises to the challenge.
