### Coaches Corner

> [!NOTE] Excellence
> Is the quality of being extremely good — superiority, eminence, or possessing good qualities to a high degree. Dictionaries also use it for a particular outstanding feature or virtue: something in which a person or thing excels.

To many, excellence is an outcome, a finish line, a result. But in actuality, **excellence is a process, not a finish line**. It is sustained effort through which we work to improve consistently and achieve mastery in a specific domain. In hockey, that could mean mastering stickhandling, edge work, or wrist shots. It is not about being perfect or being the best tomorrow. It is a self-driven standard that the greats hold themselves to every day.

Last week, we were not excellent. We lacked effort during Thursday’s practice, and that showed in our lack of heart and hustle during Saturday’s game. Yes, we won, but we arguably could—and should—have lost that game. We stayed in it only through the skill and effort of our goaltending.

I said it in the pregame chat: I believe in this team. You are all skilled, capable players, but last week, you didn’t show that. Achieving greatness requires effort and passion every time you step on the ice. You do not need to perform perfectly, but you must show up and give 100% effort every time you lace up those skates.
