This site is the public side of the Jr. Thunderbirds 18U C coaching journal for the 2026–27 season (Sno-King Youth Hockey).

Each weekly issue covers:

* **Game recap**: what we saw, and what worked or broke down
* **This week's focus**: the theme driving the upcoming sessions
* **Practice plans**: links to the plans on [Ice Hockey Systems](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/)
* **Worth reading**: articles and videos referenced that week
